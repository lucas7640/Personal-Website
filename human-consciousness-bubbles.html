Human Consciousness Bubbles

I believe that consciousness creates reality. I think that each human has their own conscious bubble that produces their immediate experience. I believe everything that has an awareness has a bubble as well but at different levels. Like the brain is a filter that takes in vast amounts of data and creates a usable reality.

Each human has their individual bubble that perceives what context they already have stored inside their memory. We get this context from data like movies, books, and any kind of story or experience. One's beliefs and their DNA affect the perception as well. Some believe that consciousness is stored in the microtubules but that is not certain or verified.

Now when human bubbles converge with other bubbles they generate a common ground of what gets built in the shared overlap so that the other consciousness can understand and interact with each other.

Movies and stories can give the brain context to ease the reality into existence eventually. With movies like Disclosure coming out, it points to the possibility that the global consciousness can be programmed to prepare for new perceptions of reality. Conspiracy theorists call a situation like this predictive programming and assert it to examples like the simpsons and aliens.

This is a fun idea to play with and understand the world in a different way then just being in it. But none of this is a tested theory and should not be taken as scientific fact. Remember to be skeptical every once and a while and have a good day.

- Lucas Edward
